About the data

The project's stated primary source is the Bureau of Infrastructure and Transport Research Economics (BITRE), hospitalised road crash injury data for 2011–2021.

The supplied cleaned datasets cover national traffic injuries, state and territory totals, and age groups by Indigenous status. Measures include hospitalisations and hospital bed days.

Injury counts describe the recorded burden. Counts alone do not establish individual risk when comparing populations of different sizes. Full dataset references and processing details will be added as the team's documentation is finalised.
