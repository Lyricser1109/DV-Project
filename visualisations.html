Q1 — National hospitalised road injuries, 2011 to 2021

Each stack of dashes is one month, or one year in the annual view. Hover over a stack, or focus the chart and use the arrow keys, to read its value and how it compares with the same period a year earlier. Press Play to watch the series build up.

Loading national trend data…

Annual hospitalisations rose from 34,033 in 2011 to a peak of 39,866 in 2019, dipped to 37,966 in 2020 and recovered to 39,505 in 2021. April 2020 was the lowest month, 32.4% below April 2019.

Counts are not adjusted for population growth, so they describe the recorded hospitalisation burden rather than the risk per person. Dash counts are rounded, so read exact values from the tooltip.

Source: BITRE National Hospitalised Injury Data, 2011–2021.

    Q4 — Hospitalised road injuries across states and territories

    Compare annual hospitalisation counts across eight jurisdictions from 2011 to 2021. Darker cells represent higher counts. Hover over or keyboard-focus a cell to inspect its value.

    Loading state and territory data…

    Queensland recorded 6,322 hospitalisations in 2011 and 10,447 in 2021. Victoria recorded the highest count in 2021, at 11,130.

    Counts describe recorded hospitalisation burden. Population sizes differ across jurisdictions. NSW has a series break from June 2017 and Victoria from 2012, so figures across these breaks are not directly comparable.

    Source: BITRE State and Territory Hospitalised Injury Data, 2011–2021.
