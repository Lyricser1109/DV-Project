Q4 — Hospitalised road injuries across states and territories

Compare annual hospitalisation counts across eight jurisdictions from 2011 to 2021. Darker cells represent higher counts. Hover over or keyboard-focus a cell to inspect its value.

Loading state and territory data…

Queensland recorded 6,322 hospitalisations in 2011 and 10,447 in 2021. Victoria recorded the highest count in 2021, at 11,130.

Counts describe recorded hospitalisation burden. Population sizes differ across jurisdictions. NSW has a series break from June 2017 and Victoria from 2012, so figures across these breaks are not directly comparable.

Source: BITRE State and Territory Hospitalised Injury Data, 2011–2021.
